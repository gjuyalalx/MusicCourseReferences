Sub-bass
40 Hz

The lowest audible frequencies. Felt more than heard, provides power and foundation.

Common in: Kick Drum, Bass Guitar, Synth Bass, Pipe Organ, Tuba
Bass
100 Hz

The fundamental frequencies of bass instruments. Adds warmth and body to the low end.

Common in: Bass Guitar, Kick Drum, Snare Drum, Cello, Piano
Lower Midrange
350 Hz

The lower harmonics of many instruments. Too much can make a mix muddy.

Common in: Electric Guitar, Acoustic Guitar, Male Vocals, Piano, Tom-Toms, Lower Brass
Midrange
1200 Hz

The core of many instruments and vocals. Critical for clarity and presence.

Common in: Snare Drum, Vocals, Saxophone, Electric Guitar, Piano
Upper Midrange
3000 Hz

Adds definition and attack to instruments. Important for vocal intelligibility.

Common in: Vocals, Snare Drum, Piano, Acoustic Guitar, Trumpet
Presence
5000 Hz

Adds presence and clarity to vocals and instruments. Helps elements cut through.

Common in: Hi-Hats, Vocals, Cymbals, Acoustic Guitar, Piano, Violin
Brilliance
10000 Hz

The highest frequencies, adding air and sparkle. Includes cymbal shimmer.

Common in: Hi-Hats, Cymbals, Vocals, String Sections, Acoustic Instruments, Synth Pads
